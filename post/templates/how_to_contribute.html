How to contribute

You can help make difficult rules easier to understand while keeping the published rules clear and reliable.

1. Create a contributor account

Sign up for a free account. New accounts begin as general contributors and can submit annotations or keep private notes.

{% if user.is_authenticated %} Browse the rules {% else %} Sign up Log in {% endif %}

2. Submit an annotation

Find the relevant rule, open Edit, and select Annotation. Contributor submissions enter an approval queue. An admin reviews each edit before it becomes public.

What makes a useful annotation?

  • Call attention to a difficult decision, edge case, or interaction.
  • Explain a practical complication that readers may otherwise miss.
  • Keep the annotation tied closely to the published rule.
  • Avoid suggesting house rules, deviations, or alternative rulings that could muddy the official guidance.

3. Keep personal notes

Open Edit and select Personal note to save your own thoughts alongside a rule. Personal notes are private to your account and are never submitted for approval or shown to other users.

Personal notes have a different background from public annotations. You can show or hide them with the personal-notes selector in the header.

Contributors whose annotation submissions are accepted are recognized on the attribution page.
